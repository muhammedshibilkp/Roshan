Rector · Jamia Markaz

Dr. Roshan Noorani

Rector, Jamia Markazu Saqafathi Sunniyya

Dr. Mohammed Roshan Noorani is a scholar, researcher, and academic leader whose work explores how knowledge traditions engage with the social, cultural, scientific, and technological transformations of the contemporary world. A Fulbright Scholar, he completed his postdoctoral fellowship at the University of California, Berkeley, and earned his PhD from the Indian Institute of Technology Madras. He also holds a postgraduate degree from Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi, and pursued advanced studies in the classical Islamic sciences at Jamia Madeenathunnoor, Kerala and Usul Academy, Istanbul.

Dr. Roshan’s work spans teaching, research, community development, and institutional leadership. He continues to teach and mentor students across the institutions of Markaz while contributing to academic innovation and institutional development. His publications explore the intersections of Islamic intellectual traditions, education, society, and contemporary change, and he has presented his research at international academic conferences in the United States, Turkey, Morocco, Malaysia, UAE, Qatar, and Egypt.

He is the Founding Director of the Malabar Foundation for Research and Development and serves on the Board of Directors of the Sheikh Abubakr Foundation. As Rector of Markaz and Director of Academics at Markaz Knowledge City, he is committed to nurturing an educational ecosystem that integrates knowledge traditions, scientific inquiry, technological innovation, and social responsibility in service of the common good.
