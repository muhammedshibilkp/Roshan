Official Announcement

A new chapter for academic leadership

Dr. Roshan Noorani has been elected Rector of Jamia Markazu Saqafathi Sunniyya.

Kozhikode: The Markaz Executive Committee has elected Dr. Roshan Noorani as Rector of Jamia Markazu Saqafathi Sunniyya, a leading educational centre in South India.

The Jamia Markaz Rectorate works toward academic renewal and excellence in quality across institutions ranging from pre-primary schools to professional education centres. The new appointment brings fresh momentum to that mission.

Research Focus

Traditional knowledge in a changing world

His research explores how traditional knowledge systems engage with social, cultural, scientific and technological change in the contemporary world.

He has published papers in several academic journals on the relationship between education, society and modern change on one hand, and Islamic knowledge traditions on the other.
